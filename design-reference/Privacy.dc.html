OPEN SCROLL PRIVACY
APPENDIX

Privacy Policy

In effect from the nineteenth of October, 2026

· ❦ ·
IN BRIEF

Open Scroll shows no advertisements, runs no analytics, does not track you, and does not sell or share your information.

§ I. Your account

When you first open the app, a random identifier is created for you. No name, email or phone number is attached to it.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple provides a stable identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email, which may be a private relay address. These are used only to sign you in on other devices.

§ II. What is kept

To keep your place and your progress, Open Scroll stores:

  • your place in each book, and the chapters you have finished;
  • passages you have hearted or saved;
  • your reading sessions, counting active time only;
  • your streaks and totals; and
  • the Daily Journeys you have completed.

Passages show how many readers hearted them and how many times they were shared, but never who did either.

§ III. Server logs

Like any web service, the server keeps standard request logs: IP address, device, app version and time. They are kept to run and secure the service, not to track you.

§ IV. What stays on your phone

Your sign-in session, changes waiting to sync while you are offline, the order of your library, and whether you have seen the Sign in with Apple prompt are kept only on your device. Share images are made on your phone and never reach Open Scroll.

§ V. Permissions

Open Scroll does not ask for your contacts, location, camera or microphone. It touches your photos only when you choose Save Image, which adds a passage image to your library.

§ VI. Who handles your data

A cloud hosting provider in the United States, under a data processing agreement, and Apple. No one else receives your information unless the law requires it.

§ VII. Deleting your account

In the app, go to You → Delete account. This removes your reading data and disconnects Sign in with Apple if you used it. Backup copies are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

“Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.”

§ VIII. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may access, correct or delete your data, or receive a copy of it. Readers in the EU may also object to processing and complain to their local data protection authority. Requests are answered within 30 days.

§ IX. Children

Open Scroll is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

§ X. Changes to this policy

Updates are posted here with a new effective date. Material changes are also noted in the app or on its App Store listing.
