§ II. Your account

The app creates an anonymous account the first time you open it, so you can read without signing up. You may choose to keep your reading with Sign in with Apple, so that it follows you to another device.

You can delete your account at any time in the app, under You → About → Delete account. That deletes your account and your reading permanently, as the Privacy Policy describes. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: Apple bills it, and only you can cancel it (see below).

Open Scroll is not directed to children under 13.

§ III. Open Scroll Plus

What it includes

Plus opens every Path in the app other than the free one. A Path is a series of daily readings, each with a short introduction. New Paths are added as they are written; we do not promise how many or how often. Everything that is free without Plus stays free: the books, the King James Version, the Daily Journey, one Path, and all of your own reading, hearts and saved passages.

Price and billing

Plus is offered monthly or yearly. The price for your country is shown in the app before you buy. Apple charges it to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. There is no free trial.

Renewal and canceling

Plus renews automatically at the end of each period, at the then-current price, unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the period ends. You can cancel or change your plan at any time in your Apple ID’s subscription settings, and in the app under You → Plus · Manage. If you cancel, Plus stays on until the end of the period you have paid for.

If we change the price, Apple tells you before it applies to you, and where its rules require it, asks you to agree first.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are Apple’s decision, under its own policies. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds a subscription, Plus ends.

What you keep when Plus ends

A Path you start is yours to finish. Once you have completed the first day of a Path while you have Plus, you can read that Path to its end whether Plus later ends, lapses or is refunded. Paths you had not started close again until you have Plus.

If we ever withdraw a Path from the app, a reader who has started it keeps it and can read it to its end.

A new phone

A subscription belongs to the Apple ID that bought it, not to your Open Scroll account, so no sign-in is needed to buy. On a new phone or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases on the Plus screen to bring it back.
