Open Scroll

Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Open Scroll is a reading app. It keeps only what it needs to remember your place, your hearts and saved passages, and your reading statistics. It shows no ads, runs no analytics, does not track you, and does not sell or share your information.

What Open Scroll collects

An account identifier

The first time you open the app, it creates an anonymous account for you: a random identifier with no name, email address or phone number attached. Everything else on this page is stored against that identifier.

Sign in with Apple, if you choose it

If you choose to keep your reading with Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app a stable identifier for your Apple Account and, if you allow it the first time, your name and email address. Apple may give a private relay address instead of your real one. These are kept with your account only so that you can sign in again on another device. Open Scroll does not email you or use them for anything else.

Your reading

  • Where you are in each book, the furthest point you have read, and the chapters you have finished.
  • The passages you heart and the passages you save.
  • Reading sessions: when a session started and ended, how many passages you read, and how long you were actively reading. Time with the app open but not reading is not counted.
  • Your streak, your totals, the books you have finished and the Daily Journeys you have completed.

Totals that every reader sees

Each passage shows how many readers have hearted it. That number is a total. It never shows who. Open Scroll also counts how many times a passage has been shared, but not who shared it or where.

Technical information

The servers that host Open Scroll keep standard logs of requests, including IP address, device and app version and the time of the request, so the service can run and stay secure. These logs are not used to identify or track you.

What stays on your device

Your sign-in session, kept in your device's secure storage; any changes waiting to be sent while you are offline; your preferred order for the library; and whether the app has already offered you Sign in with Apple, so that it offers only once. Images you create to share a passage are made on your device and go wherever you send them. Open Scroll never sees them.

What Open Scroll does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking tools.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information for marketing.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone. The app can add a passage image to your photo library when you choose Save Image, and never reads your photos.

Who processes your information

A cloud hosting provider stores Open Scroll's database and handles sign-in on its behalf, on servers in the United States. It may use your information only to provide that service, under a data processing agreement. Apple provides Sign in with Apple and distributes the app through the App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy.

No one else receives your information, unless the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app, under You → Delete account. That permanently deletes your account and everything listed under Your reading, and if you used Sign in with Apple, it disconnects Open Scroll from your Apple Account. Copies held in routine backups are overwritten in the normal course of the backup cycle.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. If you want your information gone, delete your account in the app first.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the information held about you, correct it, delete it, or receive a copy. You can delete everything yourself in the app. For anything else, write to the address below and you will get an answer within 30 days.

If you are in the European Union

Open Scroll uses your information to provide the app you asked for: keeping your place, your hearts, saved passages and statistics, and your sign-in. Server logs are kept for the legitimate interest of running the service securely. Your information is processed in the United States by the hosting provider described above, under a data processing agreement with the safeguards European law requires for such transfers. You also have the right to object to processing and to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Open Scroll is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, write to the address below and their account will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. A material change to what is collected or how it is used will also be noted in the app or in its App Store listing.
